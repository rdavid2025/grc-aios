# Decisions log

This is an append-only record of meaningful decisions and the reasons for them. The weekly `/feedback` loop writes scoped improvement specs here. Program decisions belong here too, such as framework choices, scope changes, and tool selections.

Risk acceptances and policy exceptions also go in `program/exceptions.md`, which carries the approver and expiry date. Log the reasoning here and the tracking there.

**Format for each entry:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD - Short title

**Decision:** what was decided.
**Why:** the reasoning, the constraints, and what would change your mind.
**Alternatives considered:** what else was on the table.
**Owner:** who is accountable.
**Evidence:** sources/... or the register row this relates to.
```

Keep entries short. Capture the *why*, not just the *what*. Never edit or delete a past entry. If a later decision replaces an earlier one, add a new entry that references it.
