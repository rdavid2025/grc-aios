# Risk register

`/start` fills this from question G7. After that, regular risk reviews and `/feedback` keep it current. Base each risk on real evidence such as incidents, near misses, audit findings, pen test results, or customer questionnaires. The rating scale and risk appetite are in [governance.md](governance.md).

| ID | Risk | Owner | Likelihood | Impact | Rating | Treatment | Status | Next review | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Treatment:** `mitigate`, `transfer`, `avoid`, or `accept`. An accepted risk also needs a row in [exceptions.md](exceptions.md) with an approver and an expiry date.
**Status:** `open`, `treating`, `monitoring`, `closed`.
**Rule:** The AI OS may draft and rate risks. Only someone with the authority set out in [governance.md](governance.md) can accept one.
