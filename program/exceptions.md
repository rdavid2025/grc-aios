# Exceptions and risk acceptances

Every policy exception and accepted risk, with who approved it and when it expires. An exception with no approver or no expiry date is not really an exception. It is an unmanaged risk. Approval authority is defined in [governance.md](governance.md).

| ID | Exception | Risk ID | Approved by | Approved on | Expires | Compensating control | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Status:** `requested`, `active`, `expired`, `closed`, `revoked`, `rejected`.
**Rule:** The AI OS records and tracks exceptions. It never approves one and never treats a draft as approved.
