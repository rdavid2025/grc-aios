# Third-party register

`/start` fills this from question G10. It lists vendors and partners that could materially affect data, operations, or compliance.

| ID | Vendor | Service | Data access | Tier | Owner | Last assessed | Next review | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Tier:** `1` critical (sensitive data, or the business stops without them), `2` important, `3` low. Review frequency for each tier is set in [governance.md](governance.md).
**Status:** `active`, `onboarding`, `under review`, `offboarded`.
