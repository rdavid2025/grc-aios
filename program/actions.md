# Action tracker

A single list of every open action: fixes for gaps, discovery tasks for unknowns found during `/start`, audit preparation, and follow-ups from reviews. Every action needs an owner and a due date. Escalation rules are in [governance.md](governance.md).

| ID | Action | Type | Owner | Due | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Type:** `remediation`, `discovery`, `audit-prep`, `review`, `improvement`.
**Status:** `open`, `in progress`, `blocked`, `done`, `cancelled`.
**Due:** `YYYY-MM-DD`. `TBD` is fine for a short time, but the pulse check flags any open action that still has no due date.
